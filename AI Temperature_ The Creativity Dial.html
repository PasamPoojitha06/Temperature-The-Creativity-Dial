The creativity dial

Temperature changes the probability distribution an AI uses to choose its next token. It does not make the model smarter, and it does not guarantee creativity.

How it really works

Click a step, or press Play.

P(token) = softmax(logit / temperature)

As temperature approaches zero, selection concentrates on the highest-scoring token. At higher temperatures the distribution flattens. Exact behavior depends on the model and decoding setup.

Softmax: exp(xi) divided by the sum of exp(xj). Dividing by T < 1 widens gaps between logits; T > 1 shrinks them.
